Our Story

Born in the Dust.
Built for the Stage.

Formed in Big Spring, Texas in 2017, The Band Big Spring has spent nearly a decade proving they live up to their bold promise — we're not your average bar band. From small-town honky-tonks to crowds of thousands, this West Texas outfit delivers high-energy Texas country and rock that keeps boots on the dance floor from first note to last call.

Led by Band Manager and Band Leader Bobby Barber, the band has shared stages across Texas and New Mexico alongside Randy Rogers Band, Wade Bowen, Kevin Fowler, Koe Wetzel, Parker McCollum, and national acts including Gary Allan, Aaron Lewis, Shenandoah, and country legend Neal McCoy.

When The Band Big Spring takes the stage, the night changes.

Band Biography

Formed in Big Spring, Texas in 2017, The Band Big Spring has built a reputation across West Texas and New Mexico as one of the hardest-working, highest-energy acts in the region. Under the leadership of Band Manager Bobby Barber, the band delivers a powerful blend of Texas country and rock that keeps crowds on their feet from first note to last call.

With nearly a decade of live performance, the band has shared stages with Randy Rogers Band, Wade Bowen, Kevin Fowler, Koe Wetzel, Parker McCollum, Gary Allan, Aaron Lewis, Shenandoah, and country legend Neal McCoy — a resume that speaks to their versatility and professionalism on any stage, at any size.

The Band Big Spring doesn't just play music. They deliver an experience.
